ROOS STUDIO — LOGO USAGE

1. Full logo:
   - Use logo-primary-dark.png on dark backgrounds
   - Use logo-primary-light.png on light backgrounds
   - Use logo-transparent.png when placing on custom dark surfaces

2. Favicon:
   - Use only the single-color blue icon
   - Do not use the full wordmark as favicon
   - Keep favicon simple and readable at 16x16

3. App icons:
   - Use the rounded dark background version for installs / homescreen
   - Keep icon centered with generous spacing

4. Avoid:
   - stretching or distorting the logo
   - changing logo colors arbitrarily
   - adding shadows or outlines to the full logo
